## 🇨🇳 中文

这个技能从真实品牌资产出发做 UI 动效：给它 logo、产品截图、营销 banner，或者 2–5 句品牌描述，它会提取品牌画像——主色、字体气质、摄影情绪、视觉密度，8 个内置风格文件只贡献运动语言，绝不抢品牌配色。时长分两条路：不超过 15 秒只做一次生成，用户图片是全能参考而不是首帧，默认不额外生成 BGM；超过 15 秒走编号续接，第 N+1 段必须用第 N 段真实抽出的尾帧当首帧，重复调用开场流程不算续接。分镜写成 5–7 个时间码节拍、不少于 1000 字中文或 600 词英文的导演阐述，指定一条贯穿全片的运动主因，并至少编排 3 处显式连续转场。交付只有一个 final.mp4，方案、分镜、提示词等过程文档一律不当作产物交付。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 品牌图 + 2 句描述 → 品牌画像（主色、字体、摄影情绪、视觉密度），配色永远来自品牌而不是风格模板
- ≤15 秒单次生成，用户图作全能参考而非首帧，默认不加 BGM、保留片段原生声音
- >15 秒编号续接：第 N+1 段必须用第 N 段真实抽出的尾帧，拒绝把重新开场当续接
- 分镜写 5–7 个时间码节拍、≥1000 字导演阐述，一条运动主因贯穿全片
- 至少 3 处显式连续转场：共享元素扩展、形状变形、路径延续、自然遮挡、深度穿越、折叠展开
- 交付只有 final.mp4，9:16 或 16:9、H.264；质检第一位查品牌一致性，配色跑偏就不交付

## 🇬🇧 English

This skill builds UI motion graphics from real brand assets: give it a logo, product screenshot, or marketing banner, or 2-5 sentences of brand description, and it extracts a brand profile covering dominant colors, typography feel, photography mood, and visual density, while the eight bundled style files contribute motion language only and never override brand color. Duration splits two ways: up to 15s is a single generation where the user's image is an all-purpose reference rather than a first frame and no extra BGM is added by default; beyond 15s it becomes a numbered continuation chain where segment N+1 must open on the real tail frame extracted from segment N, and re-running the opening procedure does not count as a continuation. The storyboard is written as 5-7 time-coded beats with at least 1000 Chinese characters or 600 English words of direction, one recurring motion cause threading the whole take, and at least three explicit continuous transitions. The only deliverable is final.mp4; briefs, brand profiles, storyboards, and prompts stay internal.

### ✨ What it does

- Brand images plus two sentences of description become a brand profile (colors, typography, photography mood, density); colors always come from the brand, not a style template
- 15s or less is one generation where the user's image is an all-purpose reference, not a first frame, with no added BGM and native audio kept
- Longer than 15s becomes numbered continuations where segment N+1 must open on segment N's extracted tail frame, never a regenerated opening
- Storyboard runs 5-7 time-coded beats with at least 1000 characters of direction and one recurring motion cause
- At least three explicit continuous transitions: shared-element expansion, shape morphing, path continuation, occlusion, depth pass-through, fold and unfold
- One deliverable only, final.mp4 at 9:16 or 16:9 H.264; brand consistency is the first QA check, and off-palette colors mean no ship
